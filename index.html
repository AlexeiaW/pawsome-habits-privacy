Pawsome Habits Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Pawsome Habits ("the App") is a cat habit-tracking application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Pawsome Habits.

Information We Collect

Pawsome Habits does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

The App does not use:

  • Advertising
  • Analytics services
  • Third-party tracking services
  • User accounts or authentication
  • A remote server or backend to collect your data

Information Stored on Your Device

Pawsome Habits may store information you enter into the App, such as information about your cats, habits, and habit-tracking information.

This information is stored locally on your device using the App's local storage. We do not transmit this information to our servers because Pawsome Habits does not operate a backend service for collecting this information.

If you uninstall the App or otherwise clear its local application data, locally stored information may be deleted.

Notifications

Pawsome Habits may request permission to send notifications to your device. Notifications are used to provide reminders and other app-related functionality.

You can control notification permissions through your device's settings.

Pawsome Habits does not use notifications to collect personal information.

Information Sharing

Pawsome Habits does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

Because the App does not use a backend service to collect your data, information stored locally in the App is not routinely transmitted to us.

Children's Privacy

Pawsome Habits does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App does not require users to provide personal information or create an account.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to design the App so that information entered into Pawsome Habits remains stored locally on your device rather than being transmitted to our servers.

However, no method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this webpage with an updated "Last updated" date.
